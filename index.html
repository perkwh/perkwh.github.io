How it works

What you keep after electricity.

When you rent out a GPU on Vast or RunPod, the list $/hr is only half the story. Electricity and platform fees eat into that number — sometimes enough that you’re losing money while the card looks “busy.” PerkWh subtracts those costs so you can decide to list, idle, or switch with a clear take-home $/hr.

  1. Step 1

    Your electricity rate

    Enter what you actually pay — ¢ per kWh from your bill — and roughly how many watts the box draws under load. We start you with 4090 / 5090 / 3090 watt guesses; change them.

  2. Step 2

    Your list prices

    Paste the $/hr you’d charge on Vast.ai and RunPod Community (check the market; we don’t invent rates). We also subtract a keep estimate after platform fees (~85% Vast, ~93% RunPod) — edit those if you know your cut.

  3. Step 3

    List, idle, or switch

    You get take-home $/hr on each platform after electricity and fees. If both are at or under $0/hr, idle (don’t burn power for nothing). If one pays, list there. If you’re already on the weaker one, switch.

FAQ

Questions hosts actually ask

How do you pronounce PerkWh?

Per K-W-H — like cents per kilowatt-hour. Not a crypto ticker.

Will this rent out my GPU for me?

No. We’re not Vast, RunPod, or another marketplace. We don’t auto-list, auto-bid, or touch your machines. You stay in control of the listing.

What is host-net?

Host-net is your take-home $/hr while rented: list price × what you keep after the platform’s fee, minus electricity. In math: list × keep − (watts/1000 × $/kWh). Not a guaranteed payout — just the hourly edge at the numbers you paste.

Why bother if Vast already shows $/hr?

Because that number is before your electricity. The same list price can look fine at 12¢/kWh and lose money at 22¢. We put power cost next to list price so you see take-home $/hr.

What do I need to plug in?

Your electricity rate (¢/kWh), roughly how many watts the rig pulls when rented, and the $/hr you’d list on Vast and/or RunPod. Watt presets for 4090 / 5090 / 3090 are starting points — measure if you can.

What’s the “keep %”?

Rough cut after the platform’s fee (defaults ~85% on Vast, ~93% on RunPod Community). It’s an estimate you can edit. Your dashboard / payouts are the source of truth.

Do you promise I’ll make $X/day?

No. Occupancy (“utilization”), price wars, and downtime are real. We only answer: at these numbers, are you making or losing money per hour while rented — and should you list, idle, or switch platforms.
